1. Introduction

SIPILO ("SIPILO", "we", "our", or "the Application") is a civil engineering, construction, and project support application designed for engineers, architects, contractors, supervisors, students, and other users involved in technical and construction activities.

SIPILO may provide engineering calculations, project records, quality-control records, construction documentation, reports, and other technical tools.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed or processed by SIPILO, why the information is used, how it is stored, and what choices are available to users.

2. Application Scope

SIPILO is currently developed and distributed as an Android application. This Privacy Policy therefore describes the Android implementation of SIPILO.

SIPILO follows an offline-first approach. Core engineering calculations and locally stored project information may remain available without an internet connection.

Internet access may be required for selected services, including advertisements, Google Play purchase processing, subscription verification, or other third-party services that require network connectivity.

3. Information SIPILO May Access or Process

The information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

Project Information Project names, project locations entered by users, notes, technical parameters, work records, and other project information.
Engineering Calculations Calculation inputs, units, selected formulas, calculation results, and calculation history.
Quality Control Data Test information, observations, QC records, NCR records, corrective actions, and other information entered by the user.
Photos Photos captured or selected by the user for project documentation and reports.
Location Information GPS coordinates and related location metadata when the user enables geotagging features.
Application Settings Language, unit preferences, report settings, profile information, and other settings entered by the user.

4. Device Permissions

SIPILO requests device permissions only when required by a feature that uses the corresponding capability.

Camera

Camera access may be requested when you use the documentation or photo-board features. Camera access is used to capture project documentation.

Location

Location access may be requested when you use geotagging features. Location information may be displayed on project photographs or documentation when the feature is enabled.

Photos and Storage

SIPILO may require access to photos or device storage to select, save, export, or manage project documentation, depending on the Android version and the storage mechanism used by the application.

You can manage permissions through Android system settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from operating.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide civil engineering and construction calculation functions.
  • Store and manage project information.
  • Maintain calculation history.
  • Create project documentation.
  • Generate reports and exported files.
  • Provide quality-control and NCR functions.
  • Save application preferences and settings.
  • Provide advertising services to free users.
  • Process purchases and subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Maintain application security, reliability, and functionality.

6. Offline-First and Local Data Storage

SIPILO is designed so that core application data can be stored locally on the user's Android device.

Depending on the feature, locally stored information may include project records, calculations, reports, settings, quality-control information, NCR records, and documentation files.

Local data is not automatically considered a cloud backup. Users should maintain their own backups when the application provides a backup or export feature.

If the application is uninstalled, the device is reset, storage is cleared, or local files are deleted, locally stored information may be lost unless a separate backup exists.

7. Photos, Documentation, and GPS

SIPILO may provide a Photo Board or project documentation feature.

When you use this feature, photographs may contain information such as:

  • Date and time.
  • GPS latitude and longitude, if enabled.
  • Location accuracy, if available from the device.
  • Altitude, if available from the device.
  • User-entered project information.
  • Notes or descriptions entered by the user.

You are responsible for ensuring that photographs and location information you create or share through SIPILO do not contain information that you are not authorized to disclose.

8. Advertising

SIPILO may display advertisements to users who are not subscribed to the PRO service.

Advertising may be provided through Google AdMob. AdMob may process certain information according to Google's applicable policies and the configuration of the advertising SDK.

Native Ads

Native Advanced Ads

Native advertisements may be displayed inside selected application interfaces such as module lists or other appropriate content areas. Advertisements will be visually distinguishable from application content and appropriately labeled.

Banner Ads

Banner Ads

Banner advertisements may appear in designated areas of the application. They should not obscure primary controls, important information, or calculator input fields.

Rewarded Interstitial Ads

Rewarded Ads

SIPILO may provide optional rewarded advertising that allows a user to receive a temporary benefit after voluntarily viewing an eligible advertisement.

A reward is granted only after the advertising SDK confirms the applicable reward event.

PRO Users

When a valid PRO entitlement is active, SIPILO is designed to disable normal advertisements and avoid unnecessary advertising requests where technically supported by the application.

9. Purchases, Subscriptions, and PRO

SIPILO may provide paid PRO features through Google Play Billing.

Available purchase options may include subscription periods such as monthly, three-month, six-month, or annual plans, depending on the products configured in Google Play.

Payment information is processed by Google Play. SIPILO does not directly receive or store your complete payment card information.

SIPILO may receive purchase or entitlement information necessary to determine whether a user has an active PRO entitlement and to provide the corresponding application features.

Purchase restoration is handled through the supported Google Play purchase mechanisms.

SIPILO may use third-party services to provide specific application functions.

Google AdMob

11. Data Sharing and Disclosure

SIPILO does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide a feature explicitly requested by the user.
  • Provide advertising through third-party advertising services.
  • Process purchases and subscriptions.
  • Comply with applicable laws or lawful requests.
  • Protect the security, rights, or integrity of the application and its users.

Data intentionally exported or shared by the user, such as a PDF, spreadsheet, CSV file, photograph, or project backup, is controlled by the user after it leaves the application.

12. Data Security

SIPILO is designed with reasonable technical measures intended to protect locally stored application data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, and misuse.

However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should protect their Android device with an appropriate screen lock, operating-system security updates, and other available security controls.

Users should also maintain backups of important project information.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored project and application information generally remains on the device until the user edits, deletes, exports, or removes the application data.

Deleting application data or uninstalling SIPILO may remove locally stored information, subject to the Android storage behavior and any files stored outside the application's private storage.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the retention policies of those services.

14. Backup and Restore

SIPILO may provide backup and restore functionality for application data.

Backup files may contain project information, calculation records, settings, and other data selected for backup.

Users are responsible for protecting exported backup files. Do not share backup files with unauthorized persons because they may contain project or personal information.

SIPILO does not guarantee recovery of a backup that has been corrupted, modified, lost, or created using an incompatible application version.

15. User Rights and Choices

Depending on the applicable law and the information involved, users may have rights or choices concerning their information.

Users may generally:

  • Access information stored inside the application.
  • Edit or delete available project records.
  • Manage camera and location permissions through Android settings.
  • Manage application data through Android settings.
  • Create or export available backups.
  • Stop using the application and uninstall it.
  • Manage Google Play purchases through their Google Play account.

Certain information processed by third-party services must be managed directly through the applicable third-party service.

16. Children's Privacy

SIPILO is a professional and educational technical application intended primarily for civil engineering, construction, architecture, project management, and related activities.

SIPILO is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating a personal profile.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information in a manner that should not have occurred, please contact the developer using the information below.
